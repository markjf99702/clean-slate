    How it works

    • Press harder with a pen for a thicker line.
    • Two fingers move and zoom the board. Tap with two fingers to undo, three to redo.
    • Draw a shape and hold still for half a second at the end: a wobbly line, box, triangle or circle snaps to a clean one. Keep moving and it lets go. The shapes tool drags out exact ones, outlined or filled.
    • The eraser rubs out just where it touches. Pick Whole lines beside it (on a narrow screen, tap the eraser twice) to take away every line it touches instead. Your pen’s eraser end, or its side button held down, erases the same way whatever tool you’re on.
    • Tap a tool twice on a narrow screen for its colours and sizes. The shapes tool shows its shapes every time you tap it.
    • With a keyboard: P pen, H highlighter, S shapes, E eraser, 1–6 colours, [ and ] size, F show it all, Ctrl + Z undo, Space + drag to move.
    • Add it to your home screen to use it full screen, with no address bar. It works offline.
